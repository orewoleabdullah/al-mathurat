# Content sources

The 27-item order and repetition counts follow [mathurat.com](https://mathurat.com/). Qur’anic Arabic is Tanzil Uthmani text; Qur’anic translation is Sahih International via Tanzil and is for non-commercial use only. Hadith citations and gradings below are retained only where checked on Sunnah.com or Hisn al-Muslim. Otherwise the app uses `Al-Ma’thurat (Imam Hasan al-Banna)` as its reference. The English translations of non-Qur’anic duas are original app translations, not copied from mathurat.com.

## Interface color reference

The morning/light palette uses paper `#f3efe6`, surface `#faf8f3`, raised surface `#ece7dc`, text `#292821`, secondary text `#625f56`, accent `#8d5d27`, soft accent `#e8dcc7`, and divider `#d7d0c3`.

The evening/dark palette uses indigo `#171827`, surface `#202238`, raised surface `#292b42`, text `#f1eff7`, secondary text `#c1bfd0`, accent `#d4c8f1`, soft accent `#39354d`, and divider `#3a3b51`.

The sepia palette uses page `#eee5d6`, surface `#f7f0e4`, raised surface `#e8dcc8`, text `#30291f`, secondary text `#65594b`, accent `#79532d`, soft accent `#e5d4b8`, and divider `#d3c3a9`.

Session tile colors: morning background `#f1ebdf`, text `#29251f`, secondary text `#62594d`, border `#d8cbb8`, accent `#926331`; evening background `#1d2036`, text `#f1eff7`, secondary text `#c9c6d7`, border `#3e4058`, accent `#d4c8f1`.
